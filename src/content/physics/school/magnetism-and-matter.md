## Magnetism and Matter

This chapter studies Permanent Magnets, including Magnetic Poles and Bar Magnets, and the underlying Magnetic Properties of Matter.

---

### 1. Permanent Magnets & Earth's Field

- **Bar Magnets:** Core concepts include calculating the Torque on a Bar Magnet Placed in a Magnetic Field, the Magnetic Field due to a Bar Magnet, and understanding Magnetic Scalar Potential.
- **Earth's Magnetism:** Encompasses Terrestrial Magnetism, the Determination of Dip at a Place, and identifying a Neutral Point.
- **Gauss's Law for Magnetism:** States that the net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero ($\oint \vec{B} \cdot d\vec{A} = 0$), implying magnetic monopoles do not exist.

---

### 2. Measuring Instruments

- Operational principles of the Tangent Galvanometer, Moving-coil Galvanometer, and Deflection Magnetometer.
- Galvanometer modifications involve calculating and applying a Shunt resistor.

---

### 3. Magnetic Properties of Matter

- **Key Metrics:** Defining Magnetization of Materials: Intensity of Magnetization, Magnetic Intensity $H$, Magnetic Susceptibility, and Permeability.
- **Classification:** Studying the Properties of Dia-, Para- and Ferromagnetic Substances.
- **Laws and Effects:** Exploring Curie's Law, Hysteresis, and the practical differences between Soft Iron and Steel.
