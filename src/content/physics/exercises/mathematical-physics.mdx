## Mathematical Physics - TIFR PYQs

<McqCard
  questionNum="1"
  year="2013"
  questionText={String.raw`The product $AB$ of two Hermitian matrices $A$ and $B$ is anti-Hermitian. It follows that:`}
  optA={String.raw`$\{A, B\} = 0$`}
  optB={String.raw`$[A, B] = 0$`}
  optC={String.raw`$A^\dagger = B$`}
  optD={String.raw`$A^\dagger = B^{-1}$`}
  correctOpt="A"
  explanation={String.raw`By definition, a matrix is Hermitian if it is equal to its own conjugate transpose. Thus, we are given $A^\dagger = A$ and $B^\dagger = B$.<br><br>
We are also given that their product $AB$ is anti-Hermitian, which means taking its conjugate transpose yields the negative of the matrix: $(AB)^\dagger = -(AB)$.<br><br>
Using the matrix property that $(AB)^\dagger = B^\dagger A^\dagger$, we substitute our knowns:<br>
$$B^\dagger A^\dagger = -(AB)$$
$$BA = -AB$$
$$AB + BA = 0$$
The expression $AB + BA$ is the definition of the anticommutator, denoted as $\{A, B\}$. Therefore, $\{A, B\} = 0$.`}
