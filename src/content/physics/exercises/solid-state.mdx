## Solid State & Condensed Matter - TIFR PYQs

<McqCard
  questionNum="1"
  year="2015"
  questionText={String.raw`In the basic band structure theory of crystalline solids, which of the following leads to energy gaps in the allowed electronic energy values?`}
  optA="Electron spin"
  optB="Bragg reflection"
  optC="Electron-electron interaction"
  optD="Electron-phonon interaction"
  correctOpt="B"
  explanation={String.raw`In the nearly-free electron model, electrons moving through a periodic crystal lattice behave mostly as free waves. However, when the electron's wave vector $k$ approaches the Brillouin zone boundaries (i.e., $k \approx \pm \pi/a$), the Bragg reflection condition is satisfied.<br><br>
The forward-moving and backward-reflected waves interfere to produce standing waves, splitting the continuous energy parabolic dispersion into two distinct states (one with probability peaks at the ion cores, and one peaking between them). The difference in potential energy between these two standing wave states creates the forbidden energy band gaps.`}
