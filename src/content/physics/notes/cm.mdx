### Key Theoretical Highlights

<h4>Hamilton's Equations of Motion</h4>

$$
\dot{q}_i = \frac{\partial H}{\partial p_i}, \quad \dot{p}_i = -\frac{\partial H}{\partial q_i}
$$

where the Hamiltonian $H(q, p, t)$ is obtained via Legendre transformation $H = \sum p_i \dot{q}_i - L$.
