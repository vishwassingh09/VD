विज्ञान प्रश्नमंजुषा

A one-dimensional quantum harmonic oscillator with frequency \(\omega\) is in its ground state. Its normalised wave function is given by:

\(\psi(x) = \left( \frac{m\omega}{\pi\hbar} \right)^{\frac{1}{4}} \exp\left[ -\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}x^2 \right]\)

The frequency is suddenly increased to \(2\omega\). The probability of finding the particle in its new ground state is:
  1. \(\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\)
  2. \(\left( \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3} \right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\)
  3. \(\frac{2}{3}\)
  4. \(\left( \frac{3}{2\sqrt{2}} \right)^{\frac{1}{2}}\)
